# ControlWave

## Overview

ControlWave is a WebUI that makes WaveGen more convenient to control. Unlike ControlNet, WaveGen natively supports various controls and doesn't require training additional neural blocks like ControlNet does. Therefore, this is just a WebUI.

ControlWave is mainly divided into two parts, one concerning the core space and one concerning the pixel layer. It's like an inner world and an outer world.
