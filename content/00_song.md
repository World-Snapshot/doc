# Preface

Welcome to this project called World Snapshot, which aims to record the world information for AI learning.

This project originally stemmed from my thoughts while processing sign language data: why do we keep processing data? Because models don't understand the world, and models need our help.

So why not train models on the world itself? The answer is that it's impossible. Then I suddenly remembered a science fiction novel I had read in which a civilization saved the future by storing the parameters of the world at a single moment in time.

But isn't that what so many AI researchers are doing? We clean data, use multiple encoders, perform multimodal fusion, create motion and spatial data, run simulations... isn't that right? ...

BOOM! 

A feeling descended upon me - can the world really not be trained so simply? My mind exploded, countless thoughts dancing, and as the chaos gradually settled.

Then, a poem was born:


### The Song of Computer Vision (2025)

<p>
Without storing multimodal information in basic units,<br>
When will the development of multimodal fusion methods ever end?
</p>
<p>
Without letting models understand depth,<br>
How will hallucinations and spatiotemporal inconsistencies ever cease?
</p>
<p>
If models don't know the existence of skeletons,<br>
When will models that distort human pixels stop training?
</p>
<p>
If laws of motion cannot be revealed,<br>
How can generated videos possibly be coherent?
</p>
<p>
If objects have no mass and boundaries,<br>
What difference do creatures and stones make in a model's view?
</p>
<p>
When sounds have no owner and distances no relation,<br>
Does the model truly know what the world is?
</p>
<p>
If basic units remain unchanged,<br>
Are models not truly two-dimensional beings?
</p>
<p>
I say:<br>
The old models shall die, and new models shall be born.
</p>
