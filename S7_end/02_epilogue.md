# Epilogue

I am honored that you have reached this point, having read through this rough and new born work.

When I was conceiving this work, it evolved and updated continuously. Therefore, there are some slight inconsistencies or lack of flow in certain areas (model design, writing logic). I kept some of these elements to help others better understand my journey of thought. After all, this is a document - having the thinking process visible makes it easier for people to read. And this is crucial for an open-source work that is destined to have far-reaching influence in the future. 

I also admit that I was unfamiliar with many concepts before, such as point clouds (I only started reading about voxels and point clouds definition after creating world pixels). Therefore, I start learning the knowledge only when I needed to use it, which perhaps is why I could break free from existing frameworks and explore the new methodology freely.

I am also grateful that wisdom and inspiration have favored me, during that first BOOM week, endless knowledge poured into my mind, countless inspirations emerged, and I couldn't sleep without writing them down. That feeling was even painful. Fortunately, I completed my mission.

I just hope this path is the correct road leading to the future.
